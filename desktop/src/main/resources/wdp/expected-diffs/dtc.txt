# Where the Planner's DTC page differs from Weapon Delivery Planner on purpose, for `--wdppagetest dtc`.
# One line each: <D-number> <column> <property, or Section/Key of the saved file; * = the whole column> <why>
# Columns: p / p_load / p_ui / p_save are the page, out_text the saved file (compared key by key), the rest the model.
# Globs: * any run of characters, ? one. The D- and A-numbers are the plan's (WDP438-PLAN.md, sections A and D).

A1  p*        cboTbl?_Thr?.L   the HARM lists are the app's curated/harm.json (Dash-34 4.38, Training Manual 4.38.1), not WDP's HarmList.ini
A1  p*        cboTbl?_Thr?.I   (the same: an index into the app's list)
A1  p*        cboTbl?_Thr?.T   (the same: the system's name as the app's list gives it)
D12 p*        lblTbl?_Thr?.T   HARM symbols from the app's list; a code in no list shows its number, not "Not in List"
D11 p*        mxtILS_FREQ.T    ILS frequencies from 108.10 are kept (WDP raised them to 109.00)
D11 out_text  COMMS/ILS?Frequency   (the same, in the file)
D13 out_text  IFF/*Code        IFF codes keep their leading zeros (WDP wrote 0554 as 554)
D13 out_text  IFF/TIME*Criteria   an IFF time criteria is HHMM with four digits, -1 as -1 (WDP: 18446744073709551615)
N0  out_text  NAV?OFFSETS/OA?-0   no offset aim point on steerpoint 0 (WDP wrote OA1-0 / OA2-0 for offsets nobody set)
D11 cart.?_comm  *             (the same, in the model: a frequency under 108.10 goes to 108.10, not to 109.00)
D14 p*        cbo*?_Left.?     one rule for the MFD selectors: another box of the same master mode showing the page is blanked (WDP: 11 of 72 handlers compared the wrong boxes)
D14 p*        cbo*?_Center.?   (the same)
D14 p*        cbo*?_Right.?    (the same)
D14 cart.?_mfd   *             (the same, in the model)
D14 out_text  MFD/*            (the same, in the file)
D17 out_text  (numbers)        positions written from the exact float; WDP printed seven significant digits (counted by the file comparison itself)
